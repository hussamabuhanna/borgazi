Artist statement

Art as a way of listening

My work begins with observation: the shifting light of a forest, the texture of stone, the emotional weather of a moment. Through layered color and symbolic imagery, I explore the relationships between people, nature, memory, and imagination.

Each painting is made slowly by hand, allowing space for intuition, accident, and revision. I hope the finished work invites viewers into a moment of
